# Suez — one cut through the desert (30s, 9:16, no voice, no captions)

## Idea
One parchment sheet holds the whole story. A tiny ship token sails it:
Europe port → the long way around Africa → the blocked isthmus → the cut →
the short way through Suez to Asia → the full map revealed.
Device: the dashed shipping route shrinks from a continent-sized loop to a
straight cut. The end is the start, changed (same map, now connected).

## Format
F1 Machine (token through stations, one sheet): one large drawing, eased
camera pans between stations, zoom-out reveal at the end. No cuts, no morphs.
NOTE: F1 is studied but unproven with this kit (proven: F2/F4/F5) — said plainly.

## Style
Custom `parchment` (styles/parchment/): antique cartography on old parchment.
Sepia paper, ink coastlines (boil on 2s), hatched land, teal water, gold route,
compass rose, rhumb lines, a sea monster, vignette + grain. Tiny minimalist
stickmen (ink) and ink galleons. Modern borders kept accurate in relative
position (Mediterranean between Europe and Africa; Red Sea between Africa
and Arabia; India/Asia to the south-east). Colour means one thing: GOLD =
the route, TEAL = water. Constant: the parchment sheet.

## Text
No narration, no captions, no subtitles (user brief). Only diegetic map
toponyms in small serif caps: EUROPE, MEDITERRANEAN, EGYPT, RED SEA, ARABIA,
INDIA, ASIA, SUEZ. No dates, no sentences.

## Beats (120 BPM, 8th = 0.25s; boundaries on the grid)
1. 0–5s   Europe port (build): stickman loads crates onto the ship. Quiet drone.
2. 5–10s  Old route (journey, slow): ship sails the loop around Africa.
3. 10–14s Isthmus (build): land blocks the ship; diggers dig, dirt grows.
4. 14–17s The cut (turn): water line draws across the isthmus and meets.
         Silence 16–17s on the held image.
5. 17–24s New route (peak/journey, fast): ships stream Europe→Suez→Asia;
         stickmen unload in Asia. Loudest hit at 17s (the connection).
6. 24–30s Reveal (goodbye): zoom-out to the full map; routes glow; ships loop.

## Sound (composed, no voice)
Drone + sparse plucks (minor, mysterious) → riser into the cut → hard stop
(silence 16–17s) → chord stab + sub at 17s → full pad + plucks, fading.
Acts: build / journey / build / silence / gift / goodbye.
